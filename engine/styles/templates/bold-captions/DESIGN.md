# Bold Caption Hustle (template: bold-captions)

Starter template. The brand's own answers outrank anything here; its Nos become hard rules.

## The idea in one line

Giant words, one at a time, slammed onto a punchy face cam with a zoom or a sound on almost every beat:
the captions are the show, and the viewer reads the whole video even with the sound off.

## Who it's for

Motivation, sales and money educators, fitness coaches, hustle and mindset creators, and direct-response ad
creatives. Best for 15-45 second hard-hitting talking heads, hot takes and ad hooks where volume of
attention matters more than polish.

## Format defaults

- Vertical 1080x1920, 30fps. Face the whole time, framed tight (head fills the top half, eyes at y ~600).
- Text safe zone: x 90-960, y 220-1500.
- Captions: word-by-word pop. Anton, uppercase, 120px (one word) or 96px (two words when both are under
  5 letters), white with an 8px black stroke and a 0 6px 0 hard drop shadow, centered at y 1100 (on the
  chest, below the chin, never on the mouth). One word on screen at a time, synced to the word onset, each
  word scales 70% to 100% in 0.08s.
- Money words and power words (numbers, "free", "never", "now", the key noun) switch to yellow `#FFE14D`
  and scale to 140px; results and wins go green `#22E06B`. Max 1 colored word per sentence.

## The signature pieces

1. **Word-by-word pop captions** as above. Captions never cover the face and never sit in the bottom 420px.
2. **Punch-ins on every hit.** Every 1-2s a zoom change: snap to 115% on an emphasized word (0.1s), back to
   100% on the next sentence. On the strongest 3-4 words of the video, a 135% punch with 2 frames of white
   flash at 50%.
3. **Jump cuts, no air.** Remove every pause over 0.1s and every filler. Average shot 0.8-1.5s.
4. **Highlight bars.** For a key phrase (max once per 8s), a solid yellow bar (`#FFE14D`, no radius) wipes
   left to right behind 2-4 words in black Anton 104px at y 300-420, holding 1.5s.
5. **Whip transitions between sections** (0.25s horizontal motion blur), with a whoosh.
6. **Grade: clean natural, punchy.** Contrast +12, vibrance +10, sharpening light. Skin stays real.

## Allowed with restraint

- Emoji accents next to a caption word (max 3 per video, 96px, pop in with the word).
- B-roll or meme inserts at 1.0-1.5s each, full frame, max 1 per 6s, captions continue over them.
- Screen shake (4px, 0.2s) on the single biggest claim.
- Progress bar: 8px yellow line at y 1880.

## Never

- Full sentences on screen at once, or captions over 2 words.
- Thin, light or serif fonts for captions.
- Holding the same framing for more than 2s.
- Slow fades, dissolves, light leaks, cinematic letterbox.
- SFX louder than the voice, or a sound on literally every word (it becomes noise; see Sound).
- Captions drifting from the audio by more than 2 frames.

## Rhythm (pace 10 of 10, density heavy)

- 0-1s: the hook as 1-3 giant words on frame 0, 135% punch-in, impact hit. The first word lands before the
  viewer can decide to scroll.
- 1-3s: a reason to stay ("watch till the end", a number, a promise of a list).
- Something changes every 0.8-1.5s: a cut, a punch, a colored word, an emoji, or a bar.
- Pattern interrupt every 5-7s: a whip, a B-roll insert, a highlight bar or a screen shake.
- Call to action spoken and captioned in the last 3s.

## Sound

- Music: dark trap / drill or upbeat pop instrumental, 130-150 BPM (or half-time), -22 LUFS under voice,
  cut out for 0.5s right before the biggest line.
- SFX density high but tiered: pop/click on colored words (-24 dB), whoosh on whips, a bass hit on 135%
  punches, cash register or ding only on money numbers (max 2 per video), riser into the CTA.
- Voice loud and present: -13 LUFS, compressed.

## End tag

Last 2s: the CTA keyword in Anton 140px yellow on the yellow-bar treatment at y 1100 ("COMMENT 'PLAN'"),
the @HANDLE in Manrope 800, 44px white under it. Hard cut to end, no fade.
