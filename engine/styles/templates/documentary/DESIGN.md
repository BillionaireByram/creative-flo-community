# Mini Documentary (template: documentary)

Starter template. The brand's own answers outrank anything here; its Nos become hard rules.

## The idea in one line

A 60-second film: voiceover over real B-roll, a warm film grade, music that carries the emotion, and serif
chapter cards that turn one story into acts with a beginning, a turn, and an ending.

## Who it's for

Founders telling their origin story, brands showing how the product is made, case studies and client
transformations, restaurants, makers, travel, nonprofits, and any business with a real story and real
footage. Best for 45-120 second pieces built from voiceover plus B-roll, with the speaker appearing as an
interview subject.

## Format defaults

- Vertical 1080x1920, 30fps (shoot or deliver at 24fps when all footage allows it). Mostly visuals with the
  narrator's voice; the speaker appears as an interview shot (off-axis eyeline, looking just past lens) for
  20-35% of runtime.
- Text safe zone: x 90-960, y 220-1500.
- Captions: film subtitle. Montserrat 500, 44px, sentence case, white `#F4E7D2` at 95% with a soft shadow,
  centered, baseline at y 1400, max 2 lines of 32 characters, whole phrases with a 0.15s fade.
- Lower thirds for people and places: Playfair Display 600 (roman), 52px name, Montserrat 500 26px title
  tracked +10% uppercase, left-aligned at x 90, y 1180-1290, with a 2px tan rule `#D7B98C`.

## The signature pieces

1. **B-roll leads.** 65-80% of runtime is B-roll: hands, process, places, details, faces reacting. Every
   spoken noun gets a shot of that noun. Shots run 1.5-3.5s; hero shots up to 5s.
2. **Chapter cards.** 3-4 acts per video, each opened by a chapter card: "Chapter one" in Montserrat 500
   26px tracked +20% uppercase tan, the title in Playfair Display 800, 96px, max 2 lines, centered at
   y 760-1060, over a dark frame or a heavily dimmed shot (40% brightness). Fade in 0.5s, hold 2s.
3. **Grade: warm film stock.** Lifted blacks (+4), warm highlights, slightly green-cyan shadows, soft
   contrast, film grain 6%, gentle halation on highlights, vignette 15%. Skin stays natural.
4. **Slow push-ins.** Every still or static shot gets a 1.00 to 1.06 push over its duration (or a slow pan
   for wide shots). Nothing sits perfectly still.
5. **Light leak transitions** on emotional turns and between chapters (0.6-0.9s, warm amber, screen blend
   at 60%). Inside a chapter, cuts are straight cuts on the beat of the music.
6. **Natural sound up.** Every 10-15s, the music dips and a real sound from the B-roll plays alone for
   0.5-1.5s (a door, a pour, a laugh). This is the documentary tell.

## Allowed with restraint

- One archival photo or document per chapter with a slow pan and a 2px paper border.
- A single on-screen date or place stamp per chapter (Montserrat 500, 28px, tracked).
- A stat as a simple serif number (Playfair 180px) over a dimmed shot, max 2 per video.
- Speed ramps on one hero shot per video.

## Never

- Word-by-word pop captions, emoji, stickers, meme inserts.
- Glitches, whips, punch-ins with flashes, screen shake.
- Cold or neon grades, glass cards, HUD elements.
- Stock footage that isn't the real subject (staged handshakes, generic offices).
- Reenactments or B-roll that implies something false about the story.
- Music that fights the voice: no lyrics under narration.

## Rhythm (pace 4 of 10, density balanced)

- 0-3s: cold open on the most striking moment of the story (the end result, the low point, or a line of
  interview) with sound up, then the title. The hook is a scene, not a pitch.
- Act structure: the world before, the problem or turn, what changed, where it is now. Each act 12-30s.
- Cut to the beat of the music; a new shot every 1.5-3.5s.
- Pattern interrupt every 10-15s: a chapter card, a natural-sound moment, or an interview line on camera.
- The emotional peak lands at 70-80% of runtime with the music swell; then resolve.

## Sound

- Music leads: cinematic score, acoustic / warm or orchestral hybrid, 70-100 BPM, -20 LUFS between lines,
  ducked to -26 LUFS under voice, swelling into chapter cards.
- SFX: room tone and natural sound only, plus a soft low swell under chapter cards. No whooshes on cuts.
- Voiceover close and intimate, -15 LUFS. Leave 0.3-0.6s breaths between thoughts.

## End tag

The final shot holds 2s with the music resolving, fades to black over 0.6s, then the logo fades in centered
at y 880 in ivory with the tagline in Playfair Display 500, 40px under it and the @HANDLE in Montserrat
26px tracked at y 1120. Hold 2.5s.
