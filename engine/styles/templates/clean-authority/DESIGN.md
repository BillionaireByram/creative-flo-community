# Clean Authority (template: clean-authority)

Starter template. The brand's own answers outrank anything here; its Nos become hard rules.

## The idea in one line

Expensive by subtraction: slow, confident cuts, lots of empty space, black and ivory with one touch of
gold, and quiet type that lets a calm speaker sound like the most credible person in the feed.

## Who it's for

High-ticket consultants, advisors, wealth and legal professionals, luxury real estate, premium agencies,
executive coaches, and founders selling to other executives. Best for 30-90 second point-of-view pieces,
principles and lessons where trust is the goal and hype would hurt.

## Format defaults

- Vertical 1080x1920, 30fps. Face the whole time, framed medium (chest up), speaker placed on a vertical
  third (eyes at x ~620, y ~700), with negative space on the other side for type.
- Text safe zone: x 120-940, y 260-1480 (wider margins than other styles: the air is the look).
- Captions: film subtitle. Manrope 500, 44px, sentence case, ivory `#F2EDE4` at 92%, no stroke, a soft
  shadow (0 2px 12px rgba(0,0,0,.45)), centered, baseline at y 1420, 1 line preferred, max 2 lines of 34
  characters. Whole phrases appear and disappear with a 0.2s opacity fade, no word-by-word animation.

## The signature pieces

1. **Long holds.** Shots run 3-6 seconds. Cut only on full stops or real breaths, never mid-thought.
   Pauses under 0.6s stay in; they read as confidence.
2. **One serif statement per point.** The thesis of each point appears as a Cormorant Garamond 500,
   uppercase, tracked +12%, 72-88px, max 3 short lines, in the negative space beside the speaker
   (y 300-700). Masked line reveal from below, 0.6s each line, power3.out. Holds 2.5-4s.
3. **Black and white with one color.** Mono grade: full desaturation, contrast +10, deep but not crushed
   blacks, and gold `#C9A96E` is the only color allowed through (the key word in a statement, a thin rule,
   the logo).
4. **Thin gold rules.** A 1.5px gold line draws under a statement (0.8s) or separates two ideas. That is
   the only decoration.
5. **Match cuts and hard cuts only.** Between sections, a match cut (same framing from a second angle, or
   a gesture carried across) or a clean hard cut. No effects.
6. **Slow push.** The speaker shot creeps 1.00 to 1.04 over each hold. Never a punch-in.

## Allowed with restraint

- Keywords-only mode for the hook (one gold word, 140px Cormorant, no full caption).
- A second camera angle (or a 1.12 crop simulating one) for variety every 15-20s.
- Real B-roll of the work (office, hands, documents, the city) at 2-4s, graded mono, max 1 per 15s.
- A minimal stat: the number in Cormorant 200px gold, the label in Manrope 28px tracked, 2.5s.

## Never

- Bright saturated color, neon, glow, glass, emoji, stickers, memes.
- Word-by-word pop captions, highlight boxes, bold condensed fonts.
- Whips, glitches, zoom blurs, flashes, screen shake.
- Busy B-roll montages, stock footage of handshakes or skylines.
- Loud SFX, risers, or trending meme audio.
- Filling the negative space. If in doubt, leave it empty.

## Rhythm (pace 3 of 10, density minimal)

- 0-3s: the hook is a calm, contrarian sentence spoken straight to lens, with one gold keyword on screen
  from frame 0. Authority hooks on stillness: no motion in the first second except the slow push.
- A new visual idea only every 5-8s (a serif statement, a rule, an angle change).
- Pattern interrupt every 15-20s, quiet: a second angle, a 1.5s silence, or a B-roll hold.
- Let the last line breathe: 1s of silence on the face before the end tag.

## Sound

- Music: minimal piano or cinematic ambient pad, 60-80 BPM, -28 LUFS under voice (barely there), or none.
- SFX: almost none. A soft paper or fabric whoosh on statement reveals (-32 dB), nothing else.
- Voice warm, close-miked, gently compressed, -15 LUFS. Room tone kept between sentences, never dead silence.

## End tag

Fade to black over 0.5s. The logo (gold or ivory) fades in centered at y 860, a 1.5px gold rule draws under
it, and the website or @HANDLE in Manrope 500, 30px, tracked +20% at y 1040. Hold 2.5s.
